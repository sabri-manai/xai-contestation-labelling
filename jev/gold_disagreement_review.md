Jev 1.14, threshold 0.5, run of 29 Oct 2026 (`jev_labels.csv`). "Verdict" is a reading of the
Polish target line and its context against the codebook's own rules (core test, unit rule,
boundary rules 1–4, the incomprehension and affect exclusions). It is one reader's opinion and
is meant as input to a re-adjudication, not as a label.

Legend: **G?** = gold doubtful · **~** = both readings defensible · **J✗** = Jev wrong, gold right.
"Human split" = the two Phase 1 coders disagreed on this episode (14 such episodes in the 36
double-coded).

- Gold doubtful (G?): 9 — two of them (PK_DE_08:72, MZ_SSH_02:361) violate the codebook's explicit exclusions.
- Both defensible (~): 11 — almost all sit on boundary rule 1 (representation) or the unit rule (dispute in an adjacent turn).
